Tinkr · foundation

The primitives every screen draws from, rendered twice: once with the dark tokens and once with data-theme="light" on the container. Same class names, same markup — switching a screen between themes is one attribute, never a second stylesheet.

Class list per demo sits above each box. Buttons, fields and rows are real form controls: tabs and the segmented control switch with radios, disclosures open without a script.
